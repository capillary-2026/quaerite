## 4. Main Narrative Arc

### Act I: The Market Problem

Retinal vascular disease remains a durability problem. Current anti-VEGF and VEGF/Ang-2 therapies are clinically validated, but treatment burden persists. To earn US market share, QBR202 must compete against Vabysmo/faricimab by offering a meaningful durability step-up, not merely mechanism similarity.

### Act II: Quaerite's Platform Answer

Quaerite's OcuHLE-X platform combines molecule engineering and formulation engineering. An anti-HSA module is engineered into the biologic, while HSA is added as an excipient to form a non-covalent albumin complex. The design increases hydrodynamic radius and aims to extend ocular residence while preserving retinal/RPE tissue exposure and IVT-compatible drug-product properties.

### Act III: QBR202 as the Deal Anchor

QBR202 is an albumin-complexed trispecific nanobody targeting VEGF-A, Ang-2 and HSA. It is designed for durability first, with supporting differentiation in early drying, tissue penetration/retention and CMC reliability.

China Phase 1 has started. The first patient was dosed April 13, 2026 at the 0.2 mg starting dose. Through one-month follow-up, QBR202 showed a clean safety profile. Despite the low starting dose and modest baseline CST of approximately 280 um, OCT showed a 27 um CST reduction by Day 3. BCVA interpretation is confounded by significant glaucoma. A second patient was dosed May 8, 2026, with no adverse events observed to date.

### Act IV: 2026 Human Data Catalysts

The central risk is limited human data. The current study directly addresses this risk through SAD escalation across 0.2, 0.5, 1.2, 2.5 and 5 mg. SAD enrollment is expected to complete by November 2026. MAD will introduce both nAMD and DME and should test durability more directly.

### Act V: Platform and Pipeline Upside

QBR207 shows that OcuHLE-X is not a one-asset construct. QBR207 targets GA through dual C3/C5 inhibition. Since approved GA drugs validate C3 and C5 as therapeutic nodes, QBR207 carries comparatively lower target risk, while dual inhibition creates the possibility of broader disease control.

### Act VI: Company Credibility

Quaerite was founded in 2018 and has a Cayman structure. The company has completed three financing rounds, with Morningside as lead investor and participation from 3E Bio, Alwin/Yahui, Bohe and other biomedical-focused venture investors. Quaerite has 32 employees, a Beijing lab, 2 granted patents and 9 pending applications.

The team combines drug delivery, antibody engineering, operations and retinal expertise: Prof. Feng Qian as founder, Dr. Li Chen as CTO, an experienced COO from global life-science companies, and Prof. Tien Yin Wong as lead advisor.

## 7. Investor-Safe Language Bank

### QBR202 Clinical Status

QBR202 is being evaluated in an ongoing China Phase 1 SAD study in nAMD. The first patient was dosed on April 13, 2026 at the starting 0.2 mg dose. Through one-month follow-up, QBR202 showed a clean safety profile with no adverse signals observed. Despite the low starting dose and a modest baseline CST of approximately 280 um, OCT showed an early anatomical response with a 27 um CST reduction by Day 3. Visual acuity interpretation was limited by significant glaucoma comorbidity. A second patient was dosed on May 8, 2026, with no adverse events observed to date.

### QBR202 Positioning

QBR202 is designed to compete with Vabysmo by extending durability while preserving potent VEGF-A/Ang-2 pathway control. Preclinical PK, tissue biodistribution and efficacy data support the hypothesis of longer retinal disease control, and the first low-dose clinical observations are directionally consistent with early anatomical activity.

### Target Product Profile

Quaerite's target product profile is to establish at least 3-month durability, target 4-month dosing, and pursue 6-month dosing as an upside scenario if supported by clinical data.

### OcuHLE-X Platform

OcuHLE-X is Quaerite's molecular-plus-formulation engineering platform for intravitreal biologics. It uses an albumin-binding module within engineered multi-specific biologics, combined with HSA-containing formulation, to form a non-covalent high-molecular-weight complex. The goal is to increase hydrodynamic radius, improve ocular residence, preserve injectability and support retinal/choroidal exposure after IVT administration.

### QBR207 Positioning

QBR207 is a follow-on GA opportunity built on clinically validated complement biology. Approved GA therapies have validated C3 and C5 as therapeutic nodes, while QBR207 is designed to provide broader complement suppression through dual C3/C5 inhibition, including upstream amplification, terminal effector activity, C3a/C5a inflammatory signaling and C3b deposition.

### Risk

The principal remaining risk is clinical translation. Quaerite is addressing this risk through an ongoing China Phase 1 dose-escalation program, where the initial low-dose cohort has already generated clean early safety observations and a directional anatomical response. Upcoming dose escalation and SAD completion in 2026 create near-term human data catalysts for safety, PK/PD, dose selection and durability.
